# Stage 2 dataset provenance

The Stage 2 dataset is the processed Romanized SAIL-2017 sentiment artifact
published at:

https://huggingface.co/datasets/satyam-arora-iiit-hyderabad/babyshark-sail2017-stage2

The original SAIL archive referenced by the historical GLUECoS downloader was
not accessible from the acquisition environment. The artifact therefore keeps
the processed public source and its exact source commit documented rather than
claiming byte-level identity with the original archive.
